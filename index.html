Card Scramble: Viola's Diner

Chibi Champions

Chibi Champions is a chaotic third person tower-defense/brawler game, which allows you to play as three adorable mutated animals against an onslaught of enemies. Fight to defend the cure for your mutation alongside your newfound friends. Build powerful towers and gather coins to unlock their true potential.

This game won my team and I the Game of the Year award at Ontario Tech University's Games Con in April 2022.
