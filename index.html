Card Scramble: Viola's Diner

Card Scramble: Viola's Diner is a web based cozy card/strategy game reaching thousands of active players. It takes a combination of Scrabble and Poker gameplay, and mixes it with unique diner customization and a refreshing story. The team for this game was a small team consisting of 3 total engineers, 2 designers, and 6 artists. My role as one of the 3 engineers was focused on the cardplay aspect of the gameplay as well as the dialogue system. More details about my specific contributions can be found below.

Take a look at the first 20 mins of gameplay when starting a new account!

❮ ❯

Contributions:

- Implemented many of the games main gameplay features such as powerups, boosters, board modifiers, card tap/drag controls, and the entire dialogue system, including importing, parsing, and display.

- Helped ensure proper communication between the client and server to track analytics as well as Daily Puzzle tracking and progression.

- Implemented most of the UI in the menus and the gameplay HUD.

- Created UX and game feel features such as the card tilt when it is being dragged and the card draw animation.

- Contributed to the design of many of the gameplay mechanics to improve the player experience.

- Constant communication and collaboration with artists and designers to help implement their vision into the game.
