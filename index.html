Card Scramble: Viola's Diner

Card Scramble: Viola's Diner is a web based cozy card/strategy game reaching thousands of active players. It takes a combination of Scrabble and Poker gameplay, and mixes it with unique diner customization and a refreshing story.

Take a look at the first 20 mins of gameplay when starting a new account!
